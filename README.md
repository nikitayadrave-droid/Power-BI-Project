# Power-BI-Project
A Power BI Customer Dashboard (such as a Customer Analytics, Performance, or Support/Service Dashboard) helps businesses track customer behavior, retention, revenue contribution, and satisfaction
📌 Project Overview

The Customer Dashboard is a data visualization project designed to analyze customer information, understand customer behavior, and track important business performance metrics. It presents data in an interactive and easy-to-understand format, helping businesses make better data-driven decisions.

The dashboard displays key performance indicators (KPIs), customer trends, and other important insights through charts, graphs, and summary reports.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze customer data and purchasing behavior.
Track important business performance indicators.
Identify customer trends and patterns.
Present complex data through interactive visualizations.
Support better business decision-making.

✨ Key Features
KPI Cards: Display important metrics such as total customers, sales, and average order value, depending on the available dataset.
Data Visualization: Use charts and graphs to present customer insights.
Customer Analysis: Understand customer segments and purchasing patterns.
Interactive Filters: Filter data by available categories, dates, or customer segments.
Business Insights: Identify trends and opportunities for business improvement.
User-Friendly Interface: Present information in a clear and organized layout.
